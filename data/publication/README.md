# Publication source tables

These committed CSVs are the canonical, compact inputs to
`publication_tools/generate_publication_and_presentation.py`. They are the
durable source tables recovered from the controlled publication workspace.

The tables summarize already committed controlled results and design-status
records. They are source data, not rendered assets; all PNG/PDF/SVG/PPTX output
is regenerated below `build/` and ignored by Git.
